## Problem

CRM data is useful only when refreshes are predictable and the resulting metrics can be trusted. HubSpot Search has pagination and result-size limits, while customer status, deal stage, line items, and workforce roles change on different cadences.

The product needed a read-only intelligence layer that could refresh each entity independently, expose data quality issues, and avoid turning a partial sync into a confident-looking dashboard.

## Existing product

- **Application:** TanStack Start, React, and TypeScript provide authenticated routes for customer/prospect browsing, account detail, pipeline metrics, roles across accounts, and whitespace analysis.
- **Storage:** Supabase/Postgres mirrors HubSpot objects and associations with explicit sync-state rows, status history, and activity records.
- **Sync engine:** browser-driven server functions page companies and prospects independently. Large populations are windowed to stay below HubSpot's 10,000-result search ceiling.
- **Hot updates:** a public webhook route verifies HubSpot signature v3, rejects stale timestamps, deduplicates events, and refetches only the affected object types.

## Scope and attribution

Lean 360 existed before my involvement. It is a read-only intelligence layer over CRM and connected operational feeds, with different integrations at different stages of maturity. I advised on their correctness and visibility and made two focused Portal reliability changes. I did not design or build the entire dashboard, sync engine, or Alejandro research system. Alejandro is a sibling research service whose outputs Lean 360 consumes; Lean 360 does not itself perform that research.

## Reliability questions I investigated

- Which feeds were actually fresh, partially delivered, or stale, and whether a successful webhook meant the correct mirror row changed.
- Whether manual, scheduled, and webhook paths applied the same data scope and handled duplicates safely.
- How Portal workforce updates reached Alejandro, and whether employee identity and delivery diagnostics survived retries and malformed records.
- Whether source links and confidence flags made whitespace estimates reviewable rather than presenting them as verified facts.

## My contribution

I reviewed Lovable exports against integration behavior, inspected bounded live diagnostics, documented mismatches, and wrote prioritized acceptance criteria for the product team. I then contributed Portal fixes that use employee identification as the stable key and harden delivery and diagnostics, including tests. This was advisory and targeted implementation work on an existing team product.
